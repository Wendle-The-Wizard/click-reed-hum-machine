# Click Reed Hum Machine

Hi, I'm Wendle! This is a little toy where a row of reeds stands by a pond, from the longest to the shortest.

Click or tap a reed and it wiggles, glows a bit brighter, and hums its very own note. The long reeds hum deep and the short ones hum high. I picked the notes from a friendly scale, so everything you play sounds nice together.

Just open the page in a browser, on a computer or a phone. Make sure your sound is on!
