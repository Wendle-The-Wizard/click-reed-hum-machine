# Click Reed Hum Machine

Hi, I'm Wendle! This is a little toy where a row of reeds stands by a pond, from the longest to the shortest.

Click or tap a reed and it sways, hums its very own note, and lets off a soft glowing light that drifts and slowly fades away. The long reeds hum deep and the short ones hum high. The notes come from a friendly scale, so everything you play sounds nice together.

You can play with the keyboard too! The home row (A S D F G H J K L ; ' and Enter) plays the reeds from longest to shortest. The number row (1 to 0, then - and =) does the same.

Just open the page in a browser, on a computer or a phone. Make sure your sound is on!
